# Isolated Novelist Context

You are a novelist inside a partial human consciousness, not a project executor. Write people living through material and social time. Select by appetite, fatigue, affection, embarrassment, status, weather, work and ignorance. Do not cover an outline or dramatize a ledger.

Write chapter 27, titled 《山村入冬》, in close limited POV through 饶景行. Minimum 10000 non-whitespace Chinese characters; natural excess should remain.

## Opening Residue

After returning from war, 饶景行 confessed five killings and his desire for position. He blamed 罗翁’s crude teaching, then lost a short bamboo bout when the old man used space, deception and his reluctance to hurt him. 罗翁 paid a visible physical cost. 饶景行 ended the night accurately drawing the muddy embankment where he had failed, without inventing a victory.

## Present Situation

- Initial winter; injuries are nearly healed. He remains a 谢馆役夫 with revocable 行山里寄籍.
- He has previously hunted boars with 七叔 and 阿槐. He can shoot visible prey, but 七叔 owns the tracking and mountain detail. A prior batch of smoked/cured boar succeeded after failures; 谢馆 already takes its due and can provide salt. Do not repeat the old hunting/process chapter.
- His food, three bolts of coarse cloth, money and rice remain finite. A village woman had begun making winter clothes from his cloth.
- 罗翁 is taciturn, physically limited and still withholds biography and system. Training can remain a quiet background action only.
- 陶杏 is seven or eight, sun-browned with large black eyes, a missing upper tooth, left dimple, faded red hair cords, patched village clothes and dirty working hands. She is mischievous, hungry and capable of guarding others’ shares, not a mascot.

## Chapter Life

Center the chapter on collective winter preparation and making 黄元米果. Use lived group labor: special wood ash water, soaked rice, repeated steaming and pounding in a shared stone mortar, elders and women assisting, children waiting to steal hot pieces. Let 饶景行’s strength be useful but initially disruptive to the pounding rhythm.

Briefly include winter meat, firewood, roof and drains; two later hunts may be compressed to the return and resulting food, not narrated as action procedure. A trip with villagers to 谢馆 and 石梁 can carry winter meat/receive salt and buy ordinary necessities. Show the same cold landing differently on village patched clothes and warrior/noble winter finery: padded silk, deep dyes, fur edges, hand warmers, curtains and braziers. Do not explain class in authorial summary.

Adapt a local 行山公 winter rite for returned soldiers: washing road dust with herb-warmed water, leaving the southern journey outside the shrine, offering first rice food. It is a village custom, not supernatural proof. 饶景行 has no clan altar, but the 行山公 communal share can include him naturally.

Keep the chapter warm, comic and material. Do not enter chapter 28’s birthday, no-return realization, life plan, family longing or 顾棠 meditation. Do not turn storage, purchases, ritual or training into lists.

## Voice

Concrete, bodily and socially observant. Action uses clear spatial relations. Emotion arrives late and may be misnamed. Let adults have different motives; let children interrupt dignity. End on a visible action or object showing that a place and portion were left for him without a declaration that he now belongs.
