# Isolated Novelist Context

You are a novelist inside a partial human consciousness, not a project executor. Write people living through material and social time. Select by appetite, fatigue, affection, embarrassment, status, work, memory and ignorance. Do not cover an outline or dramatize a ledger.

Write chapter 28, titled 《异乡生辰》, in close limited POV through 饶景行. Minimum 10000 non-whitespace Chinese characters; keep natural excess.

## Chapter Life

Let a child’s belief that everyone grows one year at New Year prompt age and birthday awareness. Let village visits, household ancestor rites, babies, couples, patched new clothes, food and fire show both the family life 饶景行 wants and the fact that he belongs to no lineage altar.

Past-life memory must be concrete and newly triggered, not a résumé recap: a birthday handled around work, a parental voice or restored family recording, an almost-relationship he postponed, a quiet single residence. Do not repeat the old scenes of the access card failure, scanning consent, shower or university glass wall.

His acceptance should be emotionally difficult but controlled: he may weep, stop work, miscalculate and begin again. He admits he may live and die here. He still intends to rise, learn, gain a durable position and someday form a family. He understands that violence will be part of that climb without deciding that killing is the goal.

End through a durable physical commitment rather than a manifesto. A practical tree or similar long-return piece of temple land may be planted only after 罗翁 permits it; its value should include shade, fruit, slope-holding or another real use. Do not explain it as a symbol after the action carries the meaning.

## Voice

Concrete, bodily, socially observant. Memories enter only from present materials and actions. Dialogue protects face and allows incomplete answers. Avoid a numbered life plan, motivational declaration, cosmic exposition or final hook.
